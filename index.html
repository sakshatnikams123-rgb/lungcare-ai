Lung cancer information

General educational information for your project presentation.

01

What is lung cancer?

Lung cancer is a disease in which abnormal cells in the lung grow uncontrollably. Medical diagnosis involves clinical assessment and appropriate testing by healthcare professionals.

02

Why early attention matters

Persistent or concerning respiratory symptoms should be discussed with a healthcare professional. Only appropriate medical evaluation can determine the cause.

03

Where AI can help

Machine-learning systems can be studied as tools for image classification and research. They require careful validation before any clinical use.
